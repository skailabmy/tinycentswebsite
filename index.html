Small habits at 5 become big problems at 7.

Every parent has seen these moments at home. Left alone, they follow the child into primary school.

At 5Cries when he loses a game.At 7Gives up at the first wrong answer in class.

Almost no kindergarten teaches these skills. Yours can.

These habits form between ages 4 and 6, and they are much harder to change later. Most kindergartens focus on reading and counting. Very few teach children how to cope, think for themselves and make choices.

Why can't parents just teach this at home?

Because children don't learn these skills by being told. They need to see a character they love go through it, practise it themselves, and be rewarded until it becomes a habit. Doing all three well takes a complete program, which is hard for parents and hard for schools to build.
